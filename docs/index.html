Questions

Which DNA tests work?

Raw data from 23andMe (v3 to v5), AncestryDNA, MyHeritage, FamilyTreeDNA, Living DNA, Genes for Good, tellmeGen and Sano, as .txt, .csv, .zip or .gz. Sequencing VCF files (Nebula, Dante) aren't supported yet.

How do I get my raw data?

Each company has a "download raw data" option in its account settings that gives you a .zip or .txt file. Load that file here; there's no need to unzip it.

Is my DNA file uploaded?

No. Your browser reads and analyses it on your device. There is no server to upload it to, and the page's security policy blocks it from sending your file's contents anywhere.

Which thyroid genes are checked?

DIO1 and DIO2 (T4 to T3 conversion), PDE8B, FOXE1 and VAV3 (TSH), PTPN22, CTLA4, SH2B3, TSHR and HLA-DQ2/DQ8 (Hashimoto's and Graves'), PTCSC3 and DIRC3 (nodules), plus selenium, iron, vitamin A/D and B12 genes.

What else is in the report?

Common variants such as MTHFR, COMT, Factor V Leiden, HFE, APOE (hidden until you choose to see it) and drug-response genes, each with an evidence level and a plain-English note.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. Consumer chips are research-grade and most of these SNPs have small effects. Use the report to ask better questions, and confirm anything important with your doctor and a clinical test.
